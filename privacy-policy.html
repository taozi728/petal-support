Petal Privacy Policy

Updated September 7, 2026

花瓣 Petal 是一款用于自我探索的性格测试。下方同时提供中文说明。

Test and on-device data

Petal requires no account. Answers, question order, language preferences, and report bookmarks are stored on your device. Scoring and reports run locally. The app does not upload answers or reports to the developer. This version has no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Purchases and sharing

Apple processes in-app purchases. When you choose to share, the selected image is passed to the app or recipient you select. Cards may contain your nickname and scores; review them before sending.

Support email

If you contact hardwalker1212@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send solely to respond and handle your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Deletion

Confirm Start Over to clear your current answers. Delete App in system settings removes local app data. Deleting the app does not remove Apple's purchase records.

花瓣 Petal 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 7 日

测试与本机数据

Petal 无需注册。测试答案、题目顺序、语言偏好及报告收藏保存在设备上。评分和报告生成在本机完成，App 不会将测试答案或报告上传给开发者。当前版本没有广告、第三方统计 SDK 或跨 App 跟踪。

购买与分享

内购由 Apple 处理。只有当你主动使用分享功能时，选定的图片才会交给你选择的 App 或接收者。分享卡可能包含昵称和分数，请在发送前检查。

支持邮箱

联系 hardwalker1212@gmail.com 时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及你主动发送的内容，仅用于回复和处理请求。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。

删除数据

确认“重新开始”可清除当前答案；通过系统“删除 App”可移除本机 App 数据。删除 App 不会取消 Apple 的购买记录。
